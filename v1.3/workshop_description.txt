[h1][b]Auto-Inspect on Hover (Sticky)[/b][/h1]

Hover any person to open the Inspect panel instantly. In this Sticky version, the panel stays open when you move away and switches only when you hover a different person.

No clicking. No menu steps. Just hover.

[h1][b]Features[/b][/h1]
[list]
[*] Opens Inspect as soon as your cursor is over a limb.
[*] Keeps Inspect open after hover ends, then updates when a new person is hovered.
[*] Handles damaged and partial bodies cleanly, including severed limbs.
[*] Updates only when hover target changes for smooth, flicker-free behavior.
[*] Lightweight runtime design with minimal overhead.
[/list]
