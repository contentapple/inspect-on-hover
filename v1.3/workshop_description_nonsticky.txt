[h1][b]Auto-Inspect on Hover (Non-Sticky)[/b][/h1]

Hover any person to open the Inspect panel instantly. In this Non-Sticky version, the panel closes as soon as you are no longer hovering a person.

No clicking. No menu steps. Just hover.

[h1][b]Features[/b][/h1]
[list]
[*] Opens Inspect immediately when your cursor is over a limb.
[*] Hides Inspect right away when hover is lost.
[*] Handles damaged and partial bodies cleanly, including severed limbs.
[*] Updates only when hover state changes for smooth, flicker-free behavior.
[*] Lightweight runtime design with minimal overhead.
[/list]
